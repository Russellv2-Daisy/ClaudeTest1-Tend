Tend — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Tend is a personal task and money manager. This policy explains what data it handles and how. Tend is operated by an individual for personal use.

What data Tend stores

  • Your account email and password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • The information you enter in the app: tasks, dates, budgets, notes and similar.
  • If you connect a bank or investment account, the financial data needed to show your transactions, balances and holdings.

Bank & investment connections

If you link a UK bank (via Open Banking) or Trading 212, Tend uses that access on a read-only basis to display your data. Tend can never move money or place trades. Access tokens and API keys are stored securely on the server and are never exposed to the browser. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes the stored connection.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in Supabase (Postgres) and processed by serverless functions hosted on Vercel. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

Sharing

Tend does not sell your data and does not share it with third parties for advertising. Data is only shared with the infrastructure providers needed to run the app (Supabase, Vercel) and the bank/broker you choose to connect.

Your rights & contact

You can request access to or deletion of your data at any time. For any data protection matter, contact: joshrussell099@gmail.com.
